# Wallpaper provenance

Every image below is a wallhaven.cc user upload. Wallhaven does not itself
attach a licence to most uploads and the uploader is not always the original
artist — treat these as **personal-use only**. If you need firm attribution,
follow the wallhaven page and reverse-search the art before any redistribution.

Added to fill the DP-2 (2560x1440, 16:9) and HDMI-A-1 (1280x800, 16:10) gaps
across all four palettes (see `wallpapers/` and `bin/,theme.sh`'s per-output
fit rule).

Further additions filled the HDMI-A-1 panel's true portrait geometry
(800x1280 once its `transform` rotation is applied — not the 1280x800
`awww query` reports pre-rotation) and topped up DP-1's (5120x1440, 32:9)
mocha gap plus its latte/frappe counts.
